# Code of conduct

Short version: be decent, be precise, bring evidence.

- Treat everyone in issues, pull requests and reviews with respect. Disagree with the work, not
  the person. No harassment, no slurs, no personal attacks.
- Technical arguments are settled by measurements and reproductions, not by volume. "It works on
  my machine" needs the machine named.
- kAIrOS is an **18+ project**: it runs uncensored local models and ships without content
  filters. Discussing adult content in technical terms is normal here. Two things are never
  acceptable, in the repository, in issues or in generated material shared here: anything that
  sexualises minors, and non-consensual sexual material of real people. Reports of either are
  removed and the account is blocked.
- Keep generated adult material out of the repository and out of issues; describe, do not
  attach.
- Respect the maintainer's time: one developer, no team. Read the how-tos before asking, search
  the issues before filing, and write issues that someone else could act on.

Enforcement is the maintainer's call: a warning first, removal of the content, then a block for
repeat or severe cases. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's reporting
tools or the private vulnerability-reporting channel named in `SECURITY.md` if it must stay
private.
